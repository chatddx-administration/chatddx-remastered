  isn't checked" and passes. This is by design (`dev/check.sh:20-31`), but the first thing a session sees is a check that
  checked half of itself.
- **Checks reused from the session's start look like fresh runs**: `./dev/check.sh` printed "unchanged since it passed"
  for 9 of 12. That's correct, but a healthcheck has to delete `dev/state/*-good` to get evidence.
- **`chatddx person password` only reads a TTY** (`cli.py:84`, `getpass`). Scripting the walkthrough means calling
  `People.set_password` from Python, which also skips the CLI's empty-password check.
- **The browser stage works out of the box now.** The previous two healthcheck containers needed an alias for
  Playwright's headless shell.
- **Timings are close to the docs this time**: python-tests 146 s against `docs/parts/dev.md`'s ~160 s; browser 126 s
  against ~93 s, run right after the full suite.
